
# 44. What is Hypothesis Testing?

Hypothesis testing is a statistical method used to make a decision about a population based on sample data.

The basic idea is:

> We start with an assumption, collect data, and check whether the data provides enough evidence to reject that assumption.

---

## A Simple Example

Suppose a company claims:

> The average delivery time is 30 minutes.

We collect delivery data and find that the average delivery time in our sample is 40 minutes.

The question is:

> Is this difference just due to random variation, or is the actual average delivery time different from 30 minutes?

This is where hypothesis testing helps.

---

## Step 1: Define the Hypotheses

We define two competing statements.

### Null Hypothesis (H₀)

The null hypothesis usually represents:

> No effect, no difference, or the existing assumption.
